## 📱 What is Yapping?

Yapping transforms social media debates into profitable opportunities. Our platform allows you to:

- **Bet on Influencer Takes**: Predict what your favorite X influencers will post about next
- **Stake SOL**: Back your predictions with Solana tokens
- **Win Big**: Earn SOL rewards when your predictions come true
- **Track Records**: Build your prediction profile and rise on our leaderboards

All powered by Solana's lightning-fast, low-fee blockchain infrastructure.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Browse Markets**: Explore active prediction markets for popular influencers
2. **Place Your Bet**: Select a prediction and stake your SOL
3. **Wait for the Yap**: When influencers post on X, our oracle validates outcomes
4. **Collect Rewards**: Correct predictions automatically pay out to your wallet

## ✨ Key Features

- **Real-time Markets**: Markets open and close based on real social media activity
- **Transparent Odds**: All prediction probabilities and potential rewards are visible
- **SOL Rewards**: Instantly receive winnings in your Solana wallet
- **Influencer Dashboard**: Track your favorite influencers and their prediction markets
- **Community Leaderboard**: Compete with other predictors and showcase your success

## 🛠️ Technology

Yapping leverages cutting-edge blockchain technology:

- **Solana Blockchain**: Ultra-fast transactions with minimal fees
- **Smart Contracts**: Secure, transparent, and automated payouts
- **Social Media Oracles**: Reliable verification of influencer posts
